This is not full-server measurement: independent plugin timers, EcoEnchants repairing timer, GC and JIT require complementary Spark profiling. No damage, balance or cooldown modifications.

Conservative optimization: skip custom-item identification for physically ineligible broken-item checks; preserve original eligibility and callbacks.
